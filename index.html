01 — About Me

Architecting maintainable mobile systems.

My career began in native Android development with Java and Kotlin, building foundational discipline around memory efficiency, background lifecycle tasks, and hardware sensor integrations. Over the past six years, I have applied that depth to Flutter and Dart—engineering robust cross-platform software across enterprise insurance, healthcare, and financial services.

My technical focus centers on Clean Architecture and BLoC/Cubit to keep presentation, business logic, and data layers strictly decoupled. This architecture ensures codebases scale gracefully without regressions as products grow.

I take end-to-end ownership across feature discovery, performant 60fps UI implementation, REST API integrations, native platform channels, and automated CI/CD releases to Google Play and the App Store.
